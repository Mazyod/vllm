## Operational caveats

Both caveats reproduced across two runs:

1. The fp8+spec arm has a 10.74 s first-request stall after boot. Warm the pod
   before it joins the balancer.
2. At 8 concurrent streams fp8+spec measured well below either variant alone:
   156.6 versus 375.1–475.8 aggregate. At 32 it clearly won: 1404.4 versus
   929.5–952.0.

The image pins transformers 5.14.1 because stock v0.27.1 cannot boot Gemma-4
with `AmbiguousGlobalPerLayerAttributeError`. Upstream fix #49797 landed a day
after the tag.
