## Deployment requirements and observed diagnostics

Apply the fleet environment as well as the engine YAML:

```yaml
VLLM_USE_V2_MODEL_RUNNER: "0"
VLLM_ALLREDUCE_USE_FLASHINFER: "0"
```

Keep the V1 setting for the validated Gemma recipe. The V2 FP8 diagnostic
fails during FlashInfer attention planning with
`No supported CUDA architectures found for major versions [10, 11, 12]`.
The trace enters FlashInfer's FA2 prefill JIT flag selection on SM90. This is
a different failure from the removed sliding-window guard; it is not an OOM.
The negative diagnostic is non-gating, and both V1 Gemma configurations pass.

The tested TP2 configurations report `SYMM_MEM,PYNCCL` on NVLink with custom
all-reduce and RMS fusion disabled and FlashInfer all-reduce opted out.
This is not the PyNCCL-only PCIe path recorded for v0.29.0.
The no-workaround Qwen diagnostic also boots on NVLink; its boot-only probe
cannot justify retiring the PCIe workarounds.

Gemma TP1 and TP2 each inspect 100 constrained outputs with zero corrupt
openers. Qwen's TP2 check reports zero FSM HTTP failures across 60 requests;
its short token budget does not prove completion of every tool call.

## Cost and provisioning

The H200 development session used an external label watchdog, a two-hour hard
cap, and a $35 envelope. Its compute/storage rate was $16.22/hour; inbound
transfer was $0.00395/GB. The session lasted 87.1 minutes, approximately $23.55
for compute/storage, excluding transfer fees. Evidence was collected before
teardown, and the provider confirmed that the rental and its label were gone.
The external watchdog also reported confirmed teardown.

Before the H200 session, a matching H100 PCIe offer marked deverified was tried
under a watched health-check budget. It failed before container startup with
an OCI/CDI GPU-device injection error. No model was downloaded or launched.
The provider and external watchdog confirmed destruction after 4.7 minutes;
compute/storage cost was approximately $0.38. Its image-transfer bytes and
fees were not observable from a container that never started.

transcription. The CPU image check separately exercises audio decoding and the
default torchaudio resampler.

v0.29.0 PCIe baseline. The PCIe collective workarounds remain in the fleet;
this session cannot establish that they are safe to retire.
