## Backend change and deployment environment

The initial development run caught the inherited `TRITON_ATTN` expectation:
upstream [#53175](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/53175), commit
`4fb767ff0a0e0aae9dd2720c47245d0202136dfd`, enables Gemma FP8 KV with
FlashAttention 4 at head dimension 512. The Hopper composite backend selects
Triton for image masks and FlashAttention for causal queries. Both Gemma R2
receipts now require `TRITON_FLASH_ATTN`. Real TP1/TP2 excerpts are replayed
by release; older fixtures and expectations are retained.

The initial run was stopped after collecting the TP1 behavior and TP2
performance evidence. Only the receipt expectations and dry-run fixtures
changed; engine arguments stayed unchanged. The complete final gate then ran
from committed bytes, reusing the same rental and caches.

Apply the fleet environment as well as the engine YAML:

```yaml
VLLM_USE_V2_MODEL_RUNNER: "0"
VLLM_ALLREDUCE_USE_FLASHINFER: "0"
```

The shipping TP2 configurations report `PYNCCL`, with custom all-reduce and
RMS fusion disabled and FlashInfer all-reduce opted out. Both non-gating boot
diagnostics served successfully: Gemma V2 booted, and Qwen without the
workarounds booted with `FLASHINFER,CUSTOM,PYNCCL`. These are boot-only probes;
they do not establish full V2 behavior or justify retiring the tested PCIe
environment settings. The shipping Gemma recipe retains V1.

These are short gate trend samples: four TTFT requests, one 256-token decode

threshold-gated. The new composite backend falls back to PIECEWISE CUDA
graphs on this workload. Gemma logs explicitly report CuTeDSL compilation of
`FlashAttentionForwardSm90` and `FlashAttentionForwardCombine` during
inference. Its cold decode/TTFT samples must not be presented as steady-state
performance or a controlled regression against earlier releases.

An idle repeat launched the same committed Gemma TP2 recipe and ran the
existing P2 probe three times in one process: **22.1, 149.1, and
165.6 tok/s**. The first request included cold JIT latency; the next two
show the warm behavior for this short prompt. They are supplemental samples,
not a sustained throughput benchmark, and do not replace the canonical gate
baseline above. The repeated launch's image/config/environment receipts match
the final recipe, and no other model process was running.

## Cost and teardown

One watched development rental used a $6/hour ceiling, $15 total envelope
and two-hour hard cap. The external label watchdog was armed before create.
The accepted compute/storage rate was $4.06/hour; elapsed session time
was 101.0 minutes, approximately $6.83 for compute/storage.
Staging ingress at the quoted rate of $0.002604/GB is
approximately $0.17. Image-pull transfer bytes/fees were not measured.
All model and checkpoint caches were reused after the initial staging.

Evidence was collected before teardown. Both GPUs reported zero used memory
at the final verification, the provider confirmed destruction, and the
external watchdog reported confirmed teardown. No paid instance remains for
this session.
