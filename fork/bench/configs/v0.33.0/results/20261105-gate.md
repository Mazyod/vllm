## Venue selection, cost, and teardown

The question required Hopper kernels and a real PCIe TP2 path. CPU checks
preceded all spending. A watched H100 PCIe rental failed provider-side GPU
attachment before startup. The next rental advertised zero NVLink bandwidth
but exposed `NV18`; the topology guard rejected it before model downloads.
Both were destroyed and verified. With no H100 PCIe offer remaining, the
successful H200 NVL pair was the cheapest fast-transfer candidate preserving
SM90 and the required PCIe path; its actual `SYS` topology was verified first.

| attempt | outcome | elapsed minutes | compute/storage $/hour | estimated $ |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| H100 PCIe | provider GPU attachment failure | 9.7 | 6.67 | 1.08 |
| H200 fabric | rejected NVLink topology | 7.8 | 7.11 | 0.93 |
| H200 NVL PCIe | complete gate passed | 93.5 | 8.05 | 12.54 |

Compute/storage totals approximately $14.55. Successful-container ingress at
$0.002604/GB adds approximately $0.17; image-pull transfer charges were not
measured. The session retained its $18 envelope and original two-hour external
hard cap. Every create had an external label watchdog armed first. The helper's
five-minute early-exit margin was used for final checks and collection while
the external hard deadline remained unchanged.

No checkpoint download occurred on the rejected venues. The successful rental
kept its cache through staging recovery and the full gate. Final GPU memory
usage was zero on both devices. The provider and all three watchdogs confirmed
teardown; no paid instance remains for this session.
