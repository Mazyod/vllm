The exact frozen image passed native GPU, audio, inference-fidelity, co-residency,
concurrent-traffic, and fresh-process restart checks on two Tesla V100-SXM2

model revisions, configurations, and measured results.

## Native execution and model fidelity

Both physical GPUs reported compute capability 7.0 and the expected capacity.
Each passed a 12 GiB allocation/fill, FP16 matrix multiplication against FP32,
native vLLM RMSNorm, and efficient SDPA. CPU checks exercised vLLM/PyAV/SoundFile
decoding, 8-to-16 kHz resampling, and `torchaudio.load` in the packaged runtime.

The [engine configurations](../) retain full 8192-token pooling/reranking
windows and the full 448-token Whisper decoder window. Whisper ran on GPU 0;
BGE and Snowflake shared GPU 1. All three native servers were active during
quality and mixed-traffic checks.

Snowflake and BGE were compared with independent FP32 Transformers on CPU using
real NIST SP800-53 text, at 64, 4096 and **8192 actual tokens**. Server usage
matched independently counted input tokens. An eight-input embedding batch
contained 22,302 tokens; reranking covered 32 documents and 9,572 tokens.
Snowflake's minimum cosine was **0.99999838**. BGE's maximum score difference
was **0.00021019**, and the expected highest-ranked document was retained.

Whisper used the pinned `mlmlaziz/bbyn-v2` large-v3 fine-tune. The independent
reference used FP32 Transformers with TF32 disabled on V100, explicit language,
and the same decoded waveforms and native chunk boundaries. The 274-case
[FLEURS](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs) validation fixture pins
revision `70bb2e84b976b7e960aa89f1c648e09c59f894dd` and verifies the English/Arabic
Parquet hashes (CC-BY-4.0).

Arabic differed by two aggregate word errors. Its high absolute WER belongs to
this model/corpus comparison; this is runtime-fidelity evidence, not production
accuracy certification. Sixteen long/shifted cases and two silence cases also
passed. Long cases combine held-out utterances and are reported separately
from the short corpus; they do not increase its independent sample count.
No transcript or response failure was discarded.

Twelve additional uploads covered WAV, MP3, and AAC/M4A, each with short and
long English/Arabic input. Each was compared with an FP32 transcription of its
actual codec-decoded waveform. All passed the declared error-difference gate.
Only explicit-language JSON transcription is qualified; timestamps and
automatic language detection remain outside this record.

## Concurrency and restart

Each round submitted twelve requests with twelve client workers across all
three services, including full 8192-token documents, batched embedding/reranking,
and long audio. Ten rounds passed **120/120** correctness checks. All service process groups were
then stopped and both GPUs returned to **0 MiB** usage. Fresh processes loaded
the same configurations and passed another **120/120** mixed requests.
Checkpoint and compiler caches were retained; this was a process restart,
not a new host or another checkpoint download.

One-second NVML sampling observed peaks of **6,098 MiB** on GPU 0 and
**3,898 MiB** on GPU 1 during serving startup and both traffic
generations. The separate FP32 reference jobs are excluded from these figures.

The ASR quality gate allows at most one percentage point additional corpus WER
versus FP32, and at most max(2 words, 2% of reference words) additional errors
per long recording. Silence must remain empty. Mixed ASR requests are compared
with their quality-checked baseline; vector/ranking checks reuse the independent
FP32 references. These samples establish the declared compatibility and capacity
scope, not a sustained-load SLA.

once and caches reused through both process generations. All paid creation and
extensions had external label guards. Final transfer/cost accounting and
verified cleanup are separate from inference results.
